How to Play

  • One player draws a secret word while everyone else guesses in the chat.
  • Guess fast for more points — the first correct guess earns a 🥇 bonus.
  • Guess correctly several rounds in a row to build a 🔥 streak bonus.
  • Harder words are worth more points than easy ones.
  • Team Mode splits everyone into two teams whose scores add up together.
  • A bonus Speed Round (if the host enabled it) doubles points for one fast final round.
